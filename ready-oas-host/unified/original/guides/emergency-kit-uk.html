WHAT SHOULD A UK HOUSEHOLD KEEP FOR THREE DAYS?

The current government approach is deliberately ordinary: enough basics to manage short-term disruption, built gradually rather than stockpiled.

COSTFree to use
FORMATWeb guide / no account

The core categories

Water

Plan for three days. Current Be Ready guidance uses 3 litres per person per day for drinking, with more needed for cooking and washing.

Food

Keep non-perishable food that does not depend on refrigeration or cooking. Choose food you would normally eat and build the buffer gradually through normal shopping.

Light + warmth

Keep a torch and suitable spare batteries accessible, plus blankets, sleeping bags or warm clothing. Be Ready notes that torches are safer than candles.

Communication + information

A charged power bank, written contact numbers and a battery or wind-up radio can reduce dependence on mains power and connected devices.

Medicines + household needs

Plan for prescriptions, medical devices, baby supplies, pet needs and any other household-specific dependencies.

Money + documents

Keep essential information accessible offline. Current guidance also suggests considering some cash in case electronic payments or ATMs are disrupted.

Do not treat this as a shopping list.
Check what you already own first, then fill genuine gaps gradually. The useful plan is the one matched to your household.
